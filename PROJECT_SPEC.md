# PROJECT_SPEC — VR Shooting Range Simulator (Meta Quest 2)

Authoritative requirements for the university project. Checked items are implemented in code; device
behaviour is only confirmed once tested on a Quest 2 (see `TEST_RESULTS.md`).

## 1. Target platform
- Meta Quest 2, standalone (no PC during play). Android ARM64 APK, IL2CPP, Vulkan, Linear colour.
- Unity 6.3 LTS (`6000.3.x`), URP 17.3, OpenXR 1.16 + XR Interaction Toolkit 3.3.2, Input System 1.14.
- Goal 72 FPS measured on device (pending measurement).

## 2. Environment
- Modern indoor range: 5 lanes (1.6 m each), booths with dividers and shelves, ceiling target rails,
  distance markers (5/10/15/25 m), rubber backstop with deflector plates, weapon counter, props, safety
  and tutorial signs, baked lighting setup, ambience audio, world-space control panels, clear spawn point.
- Everything is generated by `Assets/Editor` builders from procedural meshes/textures (no placeholder cubes
  for weapons; see `ASSET_CREDITS.md` for the honest description of the art).

## 3. Weapons (fictional, tunable values in `Assets/Settings/Weapon_*.asset`)
| Weapon | Grip | Fire modes | Mag | Visuals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pistol | one-handed | semi | 12 | slide cycles and locks back when empty, trigger, magazine drop |
| Rifle | two-handed (secondary attach on handguard) | semi / auto (B/Y toggles) | 30 | charging handle, magazine, optic |
| SMG | one or two hands | auto / semi | 32 | charging handle, magazine, vertical grip |
- Pick up with grip, drop anywhere (returns to the counter after 8 s or if it falls out of the room), fire
  with trigger (XRI *Activate*), reload with A/X, switch mode with B/Y.
- Distinct audio, muzzle flash, visual recoil (weapon model kick + spring recovery) and haptics per weapon.
  The camera is never moved or shaken.

## 3b. Weight, handling and hands (V2)
- Per-weapon handling profile (`WeaponDefinition`): virtual mass, balance, one/two-hand response, sag, sway, lag clamps,
  support-hand influence, grip transition time, recoil multipliers and recovery per grip, instability.
- One-handed: lag behind the hand (clamped), muzzle sag, sway, slower recoil recovery; rifle clearly harder to hold one-handed.
- Two-handed: two-point aim through both hands (primary authority, configurable support influence), reduced sway/sag,
  faster recovery, smooth blend in/out; no snapping.
- Visible rigged hands on both controllers with pose presets (open, idle, fist, pinch, pistol/rifle/SMG grips, support
  grip), trigger finger following the trigger, reload pinch on the free hand, hands snapped to weapon grip anchors.
- Ejected shell casings (pooled), lane scoreboards, combo counter, personal bests, countdown and round audio.

## 4. Projectiles
- Pooled, non-rigidbody projectiles with swept raycasts, gravity-scaled drop, time of flight, tracer trail,
  surface-dependent decals, particles and audio. One hit per projectile (no double scoring).
- Distant targets visibly need lead/compensation (speed 90–180 m/s, gravity ×2–3).

## 5. Targets and scoring
- Bullseye face 0.6 m: rings 10 / 8 / 5 / 2, miss 0 (`ScoringRules.asset`, radii 5/12/20/28 cm).
- Impact markers on the face; reset clears markers and decals.
- Motion: static, horizontal, near/far, random waypoints, timed pop-up; difficulty easy/medium/hard changes
  speed, exposure and lateral range; deterministic reset (seeded random).
- Tracked: shots, hits, accuracy, score, time, bullseyes, mode, difficulty, grade.

## 6. Modes and UI
- Free Practice (untimed; distance + motion controls), Accuracy Challenge (20 shots at 10 m, summary),
  Moving Target Challenge (60 s countdown, moving/pop-up targets, difficulty, summary).
- World-space panels: main menu (modes, difficulty, exit), target controls, HUD (score, shots, accuracy,
  timer, ammo, last hit), results (restart / menu). Ray interaction via XRI NearFar interactors + XRUIInputModule.

## 7. Comfort and input
- XRI Starter Assets rig: teleport (left stick), snap turn (right stick), optional smooth move with
  tunnelling vignette, floor-level tracking origin. Oculus Touch Controller Profile via OpenXR.
- First-launch help on the HUD and a controls sign at the spawn point.

## 7b. Windows PCVR and recording (V3)
- Windows x64 player (OpenXR). Quest 2 via Virtual Desktop (VDXR runtime) or SteamVR; Index/Vive/G2/WMR controllers bound.
- Floor-level tracking origin, real player height; same interactions, hands and weapons as the standalone build.
- Separate graphics presets: `Quest 2` (mobile) and `PCVR` (RTX-class: HDR, 4-cascade soft shadows, SSAO, tonemapping, bloom, probe blending).
- Spectator camera for OBS: desktop-only 16:9 view, first person (smoothed), third person (yaw-stabilised, presets), fixed tripod;
  hotkeys F1–F6, [ ]; never affects the headset camera or tracking; optional half-rate rendering.
- Targets: 90 FPS on PCVR when the headset runs at 90 Hz, 72 FPS on Quest 2 standalone — both only verifiable on hardware.

## 7c. Visual quality bar (V3 overhaul)
- Weapons: real CC0 models with PBR textures (pistol photoscan, M4A1, Micro Uzi), animatable slide/magazine/trigger parts, grip anchors authored per model.
- Hands: smooth subdivided rig with baked skin textures; poses per weapon grip; trigger finger follows the trigger.
- Environment: photoscanned CC0 props (fixtures, shelves, benches, crates, cameras, signage), steel-framed shooting tables, baked lightmaps and reflection probes.
- UI: Rajdhani font, smoked-glass panels, amber accents; every label ≥ 16 px at 0.002 canvas scale.
- Verification: in-container software-GL screenshots of weapons in hand, grips, counter, booths, waiting area, downrange and wide views (`Docs/Screenshots/final_v7`).

## 8. Deliverables
- Reproducible Unity project (generators + committed assets), Windows build/install scripts,
  `LOCAL_HANDOFF.md`, honest `TEST_RESULTS.md`.
